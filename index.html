Meet the Astrologers

Hamid Sheikh

Founder • Karak Ascendant Theory

"True astrology isn't about vague guesses — it's about precision, honesty, and timing."

Founder of the Karak Ascendant Theory (KAT), Hamid Sheikh brings 41 years of experience in astrology and continues to work as an active researcher. He specializes in identifying Time Events, the precise periods when major life changes around marriage, career, and authority are most likely to occur. His research also covers areas such as denial of marriage, along with several other fresh studies, and he is currently working on new research to further advance KAT. Through the study of past events, KAT also enables Birth Time Rectification (BTR), ensuring highly accurate readings even when the exact birth time is uncertain. He has authored five published books on astrology, and his sixth book, Sex, Conception and Child Birth, is coming soon.

Muzammil Abbas Qaisar

KAT Astrologer • Trained under Hamid Sheikh

"Every chart tells a story — my job is to help you read it clearly."

Muzammil has been in astrology since 2019. He spent four years learning the Karak Ascendant Theory (KAT) directly under Hamid Sheikh, followed by three years of research and hands-on experience, and he now proudly works alongside his mentor. As a KAT Astrologer, he helps clients understand their Time Events, including whether a marriage is likely to be short-term, long-term, or temporary, along with career shifts and matters of authority. He also carries out research on denial of marriage and other fresh studies, and continues to work on new research within KAT. He is currently writing his first book, now on its final chapter.
